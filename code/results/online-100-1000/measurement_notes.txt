FS-DBS receiver-size rerun, 2026-08-12

Formal receiver sizes: 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900, 1000.
Backend: Arkworks 0.5, BLS12-381 Type-3, release mode, one benchmark thread.
Online timing excludes setup, registration, group establishment, time tables,
receiver-set preparation, and other formula-level offline values already defined
by each scheme's benchmark boundary. Pairing preprocessing APIs are not used.

For Figures 1 and 2, each saved sample is the arithmetic mean of ten complete
online operations. Each formal point contains 20 saved samples. FS-DBS, KMW23,
Lee25-SS, and AFI06 use 5 warm-up samples per point. Li17 uses 50 warm-up samples
per point. Zhang23 uses 50 warm-up samples per point and a non-reporting |S|=99
conditioning point before the formal sizes. The conditioning batch remains in
results/phase24-conditioned-zhang23; it is excluded from the final CSV files.

The rerun sequence used a predeclared audit rule: flag an interior point only
when its median differs from the linear interpolation of adjacent medians by
more than 20 percent and by more than three standard errors of that point. A
complete scheme run was accepted at the first batch that passed this rule; no
individual timing points were spliced between batches. The accepted inputs are:

- FS-DBS: results/phase22-batched-fs-dbs
- KMW23: results/phase22-batched-kmw23
- Lee25-SS: results/phase22-batched-lee25-ss
- AFI06: results/phase22-batched-afi06
- Li17-AAGKA: results/phase23-steady-li17
- Zhang23-DAAGKA: results/phase24-conditioned-zhang23

All exploratory and rejected batches remain under results/phase19 through
results/phase26. Figure 6 was recomputed in results/phase27-model-rerun-20260812
using the verified 102,540,417-byte ResNet-50 state dictionary and the new
|S|=100 public-key medians. The paper source and compiled paper PDF were not
modified by this rerun.
